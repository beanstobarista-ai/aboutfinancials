A short guide

How to read an exchange rate

1

Every rate has a date

Rates change from day to day. We show the date of every rate we use, so you know how old it is.

2

A peg is a policy

Some currencies are fixed to the US dollar by their central bank. We use a peg only when we can link to that bank’s own page.

3

Reference is not your rate

Reference rates are not what a bank or transfer service will give you. Their rate is usually different, and fees are extra.
