A short guide

How to read a number

1

A share is not a pile of money

When a figure is a percent of GDP, it is a slice of the economy, not a dollar total. A smaller country can have a larger share.

2

Unavailable is not zero

We use the latest non-empty World Bank observation and keep its year visible. A compatible IMF estimate can fill only a documented gap and is labelled; otherwise the figure stays unavailable.

3

Annual, then one FX day

Country indicators are yearly and can lag. The exchange rates are a single ECB reference day, not a price streaming from a trading screen.

World Bank World Development Indicators (API v2): latest non-empty observation per series, plus short GDP-growth and inflation histories on country pages. Definition-compatible IMF World Economic Outlook estimates may fill selected inflation and current-account gaps and are labelled. Exchange rates are European Central Bank reference rates via Frankfurter, for the rate date shown on Markets. They are not live market ticks.

The calendar is a snapshot of Forex Factory’s public weekly file. It includes forecast and previous when that file has them, and it has no released actuals. It does not update on its own.

Every displayed figure keeps its source, source series, observation year, status, and exact value. Unavailable is not zero.
