A short guide

How to read a number

1

A share is not a pile of money

When a figure is a percent of GDP, it is a slice of the economy, not a dollar total. A smaller country can have a larger share.

2

Unavailable is not zero

We use the latest non-empty World Bank observation and keep its year visible. A compatible IMF estimate can fill only a documented gap and is labelled; otherwise the figure stays unavailable.

3

Annual, then one FX day

Country indicators are yearly and can lag. The exchange rates are a single ECB reference day, not a price streaming from a trading screen.
