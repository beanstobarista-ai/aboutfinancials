Privacy

Privacy

Short version: no accounts, no cookies of our own, no tracking scripts. What you type into Money Map stays in your browser.

Last updated 6 October 2026

What we collect

AboutFinancials does not run accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts, and does not set cookies of its own. We do not collect your name, email address, or financial details.

Like any website, our hosting server may keep standard technical logs of requests (for example IP address, browser type, page requested, and time) for security and to keep the site running.

Money Map and local storage

Money Map saves your items and your chosen home currency in your browser’s local storage, on your own device. It is never sent to us. We use these keys:

  • af-money-map-v1: your items (name, asset or debt, amount, currency) and your home currency.
  • af-money-map-rates-v1: a copy of the last exchange rates loaded, so the tool can still work if the rate service is unavailable.

To delete this data, use “Clear all items from this browser” on the Money Map page, or clear site data in your browser settings. Anyone with access to your device and browser profile could see it, so avoid using Money Map on a shared computer.

Exchange rates from Frankfurter

When you open Money Map, your browser requests exchange rates directly from the Frankfurter API at api.frankfurter.dev. That request contains no information you typed. As with any web request, Frankfurter’s servers (which run behind Cloudflare) receive your IP address and browser details. Frankfurter states that its API “doesn’t collect or log personal data, IP addresses, or request URLs” (Frankfurter FAQ).

Email updates

The email box on Money Map is switched off. It does not collect or send anything. If we turn on email updates later, we will update this page first to say which service we use and how to unsubscribe.

Links to other sites

We link to sources such as central banks and the European Central Bank. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
