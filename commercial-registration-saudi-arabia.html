Commercial registration (CR) in Saudi Arabia

Your commercial registration (CR) is your business’s official record with the Ministry of Commerce. Under the 2024 Commercial Register Law, one registration can cover several activities, and you must confirm your registration data every year.

Information as of 10 October 2026 · Last reviewed 10 October 2026 · Sources: official Saudi government publications

Short answer

Every merchant must register in the Commercial Register. You apply electronically, and the registrar must decide on a complete application within 10 days. After that, you must update any change within 15 days and confirm your data every year. Missing the confirmation by 90 days suspends the CR (Commercial Register Law, Articles 5, 7, 10, 11 and 15).

What a CR is

The Ministry of Commerce keeps the Commercial Register, and every merchant must be registered in it (Commercial Register Law, Articles 3 and 5). The current law was issued by Royal Decree No. M/83 on 22 September 2024. It replaced the 1416H law and took effect 180 days after publication in the Official Gazette (Article 29).

Before you apply

  • Foreign investors: complete your MISA investment registration first. MISA says you can issue a CR once you receive its notice that registration is complete (MISA FAQ 2.2).
  • Choose your legal form, such as an establishment, an LLC or an SJSC. Read business structures.
  • Prepare the data the law requires (Article 6): the applicant’s name, address and ID number; your trade name; legal form; capital; the managers or board members and their powers; and the address of your main place of business.

How to apply

  1. Apply online. All CR applications are electronic (Article 8). Applications go through the Saudi Business Center platform, which works with the Ministry of Commerce.
  2. Add your activities. You can register several business activities on one CR, even if they are unrelated, subject to other laws (Article 9).
  3. Wait for a decision. The registrar decides on a complete application within 10 days. If the application is rejected for an error or a missing item, you can correct it within the period set in the Regulations. You can appeal a rejection to the Ministry within 60 days (Article 7).
  4. Connected registrations. The Ministry of Commerce says issuing a CR also opens your files with the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, ZATCA, GOSI, Saudi Post’s national address service and the Chamber of Commerce (Ministry of Commerce).

Fees: the Commercial Register Law leaves fees to be set separately (Article 27). Check the amount on the payment screen before you pay. We do not repeat fee figures we could not confirm on an official page.

The yearly confirmation

Every merchant must submit an annual statement confirming the data in the register (Article 11). The rules for missing it are strict (Article 15):

  • If you do not confirm within 90 days of the due date, the registrar suspends your registration. You get 14 days’ notice first.
  • While suspended, every licence issued under that CR is suspended too.
  • Suspension lasts one year. You can lift it during that year by confirming, paying the fees and paying any fine.
  • If nothing is done in that year, a sole trader’s registration is struck off. For a company or a foreign company’s branch, the Ministry may ask the partners to dissolve the company or go to court to dissolve it.

Updating your CR

If anything in your registration changes, such as managers, address, capital or activities, you must update it within 15 days (Article 10). You are responsible for the accuracy of the data you submit, not the registrar (Article 12).

Penalties

Giving incorrect data, failing to register, or failing to update, confirm or display your registration can bring a fine of up to SAR 50,000. The fine can double for a repeat violation within three years (Article 21).

Closing a CR

If you stop trading, you can ask for a voluntary strike-off, subject to the Companies Law (Article 13). For a company, the strike-off is subject to the Companies Law, and a completed liquidation also leads to strike-off (Article 14).

After your CR, the next step is usually VAT registration. See the full route in how to start a business in Saudi Arabia.

Frequently asked questions

Does a Saudi CR expire?

The 2024 Commercial Register Law sets no renewal period. Instead, you must confirm your CR data every year, and if you miss the confirmation by 90 days, the registration is suspended.

How long does it take to get a CR?

The registrar must decide on a complete application within 10 days under Article 7 of the Commercial Register Law. The Regulations can allow extensions.

Can I have more than one activity on my CR?

Yes. Article 9 lets a merchant register several business activities, even unrelated ones, subject to other relevant laws.

What happens if I forget the annual confirmation?

After 90 days the CR is suspended, along with every licence issued under it. You have one year to fix it. After that, a sole trader’s registration is struck off, and a company may face dissolution.

How fast must I report changes?

Within 15 days of the change, under Article 10.
