Method

What the model calculates

Sales and seat use

Sales equals covers per day × operating days × average check. Covers per seat equals daily covers ÷ seats.

Contribution

Sales less food, transaction and other variable costs.

Operating result

Contribution less the five fixed-cost inputs. This is not net income or cash flow.

Break-even sales

Total fixed costs ÷ contribution-margin ratio. The result is unavailable when the ratio is zero or negative.

Break-even covers

Break-even monthly sales ÷ operating days ÷ average check.

Opening funding

Opening investment plus working-capital reserve. Financing costs are not included.

Before relying on the result

Complete the local evidence pack

01

Demand

Footfall or delivery-zone evidence, service-period demand, competitor pricing and a documented covers build-up.

02

Site

Lease proposal, permitted use, occupancy limits, fit-out quotations, utilities capacity and accessibility requirements.

03

Labour

Staffing by shift, applicable state and local minimum wages, tip rules, payroll taxes, benefits and overtime.

04

Menu

Recipe-level ingredient quantities, supplier quotes, yield, waste, packaging and menu-mix evidence.

05

Compliance

Entity registration, food-establishment permits, health review, fire and building approvals, liquor rules and sales tax.

06

Funding

Fit-out schedule, deposit timing, contingency, pre-opening payroll, working-capital runway and financing terms.

Official context used in this model

These sources shape the planning and compliance framework. They do not supply any of the financial assumptions entered above.

U.S. Small Business Administration · Plan your business

Publisher: SBA · Scope: U.S. business-plan structure, startup-cost and break-even guidance · Retrieved 5 October 2026 · Update cadence: irregular · Units: none used · Missing-data behavior: contributes no financial value.

U.S. Census Bureau · 2022 NAICS restaurant classifications

Publisher: U.S. Census Bureau · Scope: U.S. industry classification, including 722511 full-service restaurants and 722513 limited-service restaurants · Retrieved 5 October 2026 · Update cadence: periodic NAICS revisions · Units: none · Missing-data behavior: user selects “other” when neither classification fits.

U.S. Department of Labor · Minimum-wage questions and answers

Publisher: U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division · Scope: federal wage floor and the requirement to apply a higher state minimum where applicable · Retrieved 5 October 2026 · Update cadence: rule-dependent · Units: no wage value imported · Missing-data behavior: local wage input remains required.

U.S. Department of Labor · Fact Sheet 15, tipped employees

Publisher: U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division · Scope: federal tipped-employee framework · Retrieved 5 October 2026 · Update cadence: rule-dependent · Geography: United States, subject to stricter state and local law · Units: no wage value imported · Missing-data behavior: user payroll assumption must reflect the chosen jurisdiction.

U.S. Food and Drug Administration · FDA Food Code

Publisher: FDA · Current page identifies the 2026 Food Code as the most recent full edition · Scope: model food-safety code for adoption by jurisdictions · Retrieved 5 October 2026 · Update cadence: full editions plus interim supplements · Units: none · Missing-data behavior: local adoption and permit requirements remain unresolved until the jurisdiction is researched.
