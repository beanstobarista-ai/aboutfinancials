About AboutFinancials

Finance, made clear, for people whose money lives in more than one country or currency.

Who it’s for

Expats, Gulf residents, remote workers, and freelancers paid from abroad. If you earn in one currency and save, borrow, or support family in another, this site is for you.

What we make

  • Free tools. The first is Money Map, which adds up what you own and owe across currencies. It runs in your browser.
  • Explainers. Short, interactive pages on money ideas such as exchange rates and currency pegs. Coming soon.
  • Templates. Spreadsheets built for more than one currency. Coming soon. If we sell them, the price will be shown clearly before you buy.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t give financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.
  • We don’t recommend specific banks, brokers, or products.
  • We don’t ask for bank logins or connect to your accounts.
  • We don’t publish a number unless we can show where it came from.

Who publishes this site

AboutFinancials is the publisher of this website. How we source numbers and use AI tools is explained in our editorial and AI policy.
