Sector plans built from operating drivers, local evidence, and transparent assumptions—not prefabricated projections pretending to fit every country.

Models are not forecasts. A plan receives numbers only after its location, currency, scale, date and operating assumptions are defined. Until then, the input remains unavailable rather than being filled with an invented industry average.

Food and agriculture

Broiler chicken farm

Awaiting location

The commercial result is driven by biology, feed economics, cycle timing, farm capacity and the route to market.

Core operating inputs

  • Chick cost, stocking density and usable housing capacity
  • Feed conversion, feed price, mortality and cycle length
  • Veterinary, bedding, water, energy and labour costs
  • Live-weight or processed selling price and offtake terms
  • Biosecurity, downtime, licensing and disposal requirements

No financial output has been calculated. Geography, currency, production model and source set must be approved first.

Food service

Restaurant

US model available

The commercial result is driven by demand by service period, capacity use, menu economics, staffing and occupancy cost.

Core operating inputs

  • Seats, covers, table turns, opening hours and average check
  • Menu mix, ingredient cost, wastage and packaging
  • Kitchen and service staffing by shift
  • Rent, fit-out, utilities and delivery-platform commissions
  • Licences, tax, payment fees and working-capital timing

Start with the U.S. model, enter a city and local assumptions, then inspect monthly economics, break-even and your own downside and upside cases.

Model architecture

From evidence to a decision

1

Define

Country, city, currency, date, customer, scale and operating format.

2

Source

Official rules and statistics, supplier quotations, leases and documented market evidence.

3

Model

Unit economics, capacity, staffing, investment, working capital and monthly cash flow.

4

Stress

Break-even, downside and upside cases with every changed assumption visible.

Evidence labels

Every number must declare what it is

External evidence

Source fact

A value from a named official source, contract, quotation or documented market publication.

Owner input

User assumption

A proposed price, volume, capacity, wage or operating choice supplied for the model.

Calculated

Model result

An output reproducible from identified source facts and user assumptions.

Future downloadable plans will include an assumption register, source links, calculation logic, version date and sensitivity table. Local licensing, tax and safety requirements will be checked for the specified jurisdiction before publication.
