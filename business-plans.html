Model architecture

From evidence to a decision

1

Define

Country, city, currency, date, customer, scale and operating format.

2

Source

Official rules and statistics, supplier quotations, leases and documented market evidence.

3

Model

Unit economics, capacity, staffing, investment, working capital and monthly cash flow.

4

Stress

Break-even, downside and upside cases with every changed assumption visible.

Evidence labels

Every number must declare what it is

External evidence

Source fact

A value from a named official source, contract, quotation or documented market publication.

Owner input

User assumption

A proposed price, volume, capacity, wage or operating choice supplied for the model.

Calculated

Model result

An output reproducible from identified source facts and user assumptions.

Future downloadable plans will include an assumption register, source links, calculation logic, version date and sensitivity table. Local licensing, tax and safety requirements will be checked for the specified jurisdiction before publication.
