Founder stories

How Saudi businesses really started

Not success-story gloss. We ask founders what it cost to open, what took longer than planned, what they got wrong and what they would do differently. The numbers they share help everyone plan better.

The first stories are being recorded now. Each one is published only after the founder has reviewed it. If you run a business in Saudi Arabia, or know someone who does, tell us below.

The paperwork

What they would change if they opened again tomorrow.
