Where the rates come from

Most rates are European Central Bank euro reference rates, loaded when you open this page through the free Frankfurter API. Source: ECB statistics. Conversions between two non-euro currencies are calculated through the euro.

The ECB does not publish rates for Gulf currencies. The ones below are fixed to the US dollar by their central bank. We include each one only with a link to that central bank’s own page, and convert it through the ECB’s US dollar rate.

    These are reference rates for understanding your position. They are not quotes. A bank or transfer service will use its own rate and may charge fees. The Kuwaiti dinar is not included because it is managed against a basket of currencies rather than a single published peg.
