How to start a home business in Saudi Arabia

Some of the Kingdom’s best-known food and craft brands started in a family kitchen or a spare room. The government has built specific routes for exactly that: productive family certificates and home-based licences for people who make things at home, and the freelance document for people who sell their skills. Choosing the right route early decides what you can sell, where you can sell it and how easily you can grow.

Information as of 12 October 2026 · Checked against official Saudi sources · About 13 min read

At a glance

  • Making products at home (food, sweets, crafts): the productive families framework. The Social Development Bank issues a productive family certificate online, and municipalities issue home-based business licences under the productive families regulations.
  • Selling your skills (design, writing, programming, consulting and other approved professions): the HRSD freelance document, open to Saudi citizens aged 18 to 60, valid for one year and renewable, issued free through the freelance platform.
  • Selling online: store authentication on the Saudi Business Center’s Business Platform needs a CR or a valid freelance document plus a commercial bank account linked to the store.
  • Food from home carries extra responsibilities: municipal inspection of the space, and food safety standards set by the SFDA.
  • Tax still applies: VAT becomes mandatory above SAR 375,000 of annual taxable supplies.

Who this guide is for

This guide is for Saudi citizens and families who want to earn from home, whether that means baking, cooking, sewing, perfume blending, handicrafts, tutoring, design or professional services. It explains the official routes, how they differ, what each lets you do, and how to move into a full business when you outgrow the kitchen table. If you plan to sell mainly through a website or social media, read it together with our online business guide.

The routes described here are designed for Saudi citizens. Residents who are not Saudi generally need a full business set-up, starting with MISA registration, as described in our main set-up guide and our MISA guide.

The productive families route

The Social Development Bank certificate

The Social Development Bank runs a fully online service that issues a productive family certificate. In the bank’s words, it is designed to help families turn home-based businesses into formal projects: the certificate grants the regulatory licence to practise the activity and qualifies the family for financial support, development services and consultation. No branch visit is needed. The bank also runs a proactive renewal service: certificates linked to active productive family financing renew automatically once the renewal requirements are verified, and families without financing are notified before expiry and can renew online.

For most families making food or crafts, this certificate is the natural starting point, because it puts the business on a formal footing and opens the door to the bank’s financing programmes.

The municipal home-based business licence

Municipalities issue home-based business licences under the productive families regulations. Riyadh’s municipality, for example, describes the service as issuing a productive family licence to conduct commercial activities in line with those regulations. The process runs: an online application with attachments, review by the licensing department, a scheduled site visit in which an inspector checks the space against the activity requirements, then approvals up to the mayor, after which the licence is issued electronically. Riyadh quotes three working days and lists the service as free. Other cities run their own versions, so check your municipality’s portal and use Balady’s activity requirements inquiry to see what the space must meet for your activity.

Food from home

Food is the most popular home business and the one with the highest responsibility. Expect the inspection to focus on the preparation area: cleanliness, separation from household use, storage, and pest control. Treat SFDA food safety standards as your baseline even at small volumes: buy ingredients from reliable suppliers, keep cold food cold, label products with ingredients, allergens and dates, and package properly for transport. If you sell through cafés, shops or delivery apps, those partners will increasingly expect to see your paperwork before they list you.

The freelance document route

The freelance document, which HRSD calls the self-employment permit, is for people who work for their own account rather than for a single employer. It is issued through the official freelance platform. A ministerial decision sets the basics: Saudi citizens aged 18 to 60 can apply, and the document is valid for one year and renewable for similar periods. HRSD lists the service as free.

How to apply

  1. Log in to the freelance platform through the national single sign-on (Nafath).
  2. Choose “Issue Freelance Document”, then the category and the profession.
  3. Upload the attachments required for that profession and accept the terms.
  4. Submit, and wait while the operations team reviews the attachments.
  5. Once approved, download the document as a PDF from your dashboard.

Your dashboard shows the status of each document: active, expired, pending, suspended or rejected. Rejected requests show the reason and can be corrected and resubmitted. Anyone can verify a document through the platform’s certificate check, which is useful when a corporate client asks for proof.

What the document does and does not do

The document is proof that you are an approved self-employed professional in a listed profession. It does not find you clients, it does not cover professions outside the platform’s list, and it does not replace sector licences where a profession needs one. If your activity is not on the list, you need a commercial registration instead. Food trucks have their own path: the Balady mobile cart licence issues a freelance certificate automatically, as our food truck guide explains.

Selling online from home

Most home businesses now sell through Instagram, WhatsApp, a store builder or delivery apps. Two sets of rules matter:

  • Store authentication. The Ministry of Commerce moved online store authentication from Maroof to the Saudi Business Center’s Business Platform in 2023, and says the Business Platform is the only approved channel. To be authenticated you need a valid CR or a valid freelance document, plus a commercial bank account linked to the store. The ministry warns consumers against transferring money to personal accounts, so customers increasingly look for authenticated stores.
  • The E-Commerce Law. Anyone selling to consumers online must disclose their identity and contact details (unless registered with an authentication entity), give clear terms with the total price including taxes and delivery, issue an itemised invoice and respect the consumer’s seven-day cancellation right where it applies. Custom-made products are among the exceptions.

Our online business guide covers these rules in detail.

Money, tax and records

  • VAT. Small home businesses are usually well under the thresholds, but the rules still apply: registration is mandatory once annual taxable supplies exceed SAR 375,000 and optional from SAR 187,500. See our VAT guide.
  • Separate accounts. Keep business money apart from household money. Store authentication requires a commercial bank account anyway, and clean records make financing applications far easier.
  • Price properly. Home businesses often under-price because they ignore their own time, utilities and packaging. Cost every product fully, including delivery and platform fees.

Step by step

  1. Define what you sell and whether it is a product made at home or a service.
  2. Pick the route: productive family certificate and, where the activity needs it, a municipal home-based licence for products; the freelance document for services.
  3. Check the activity requirements on Balady before preparing the space.
  4. Apply online and prepare for the site inspection if your activity requires one.
  5. Open a business bank account and, if you sell online, authenticate your store on the Business Platform.
  6. Set up simple bookkeeping from day one and track rolling twelve-month sales against the VAT thresholds.
  7. Renew on time: the freelance document is valid for a year, and productive family certificates have their own renewal cycle.

When to move to a full business

Signs that you have outgrown the home route: you want to hire staff, you need a production space larger than your home allows, a major retailer wants you as a supplier, you want a partner or investor, or your sales are approaching the VAT threshold. At that point a commercial registration and a proper legal form usually make sense. Our business structures guide explains the options, and our café guide shows what moving into a shop involves. The Companies Law also allows the owner of a sole proprietorship to transfer its assets into a company later.

Choosing what to sell from home

The best home businesses share three traits: a product people already ask you for, a production process that fits safely in the space you have, and margins that still work once you count your own time. Before you formalise anything, test demand with friends, family and a small social media audience. Track what sells, what customers say and how long each order really takes you.

Then match the product to the route. Physical products made at home, especially food, fit the productive families framework. Skills and services sold to clients fit the freelance document. If what you sell needs a sector licence, for example health, education or financial services, check that licence first, because neither route replaces it.

Sales channels and building a brand

  • Social media and messaging. The fastest way to start. Keep prices, terms and delivery fees clear in every post, because online advertisements bind the seller under the E-Commerce Law.
  • Your own online store. More professional and easier to scale. Authenticate it on the Business Platform and display the authentication.
  • Outlets and partners. Cafés, gift shops and bazaars want reliable supply and proper paperwork. Have your certificate, labels and invoices ready.
  • Delivery apps. Useful for food, but check each platform’s requirements and commission before committing your margins.

Whichever channel you use, invest early in a consistent name, simple packaging and clear labelling. Make sure the brand is registered to you, not to a relative or a friend’s account, so it can move with you when the business grows.

Pricing a home product properly

Under-pricing is the most common reason home businesses stall. Price each product from the bottom up: ingredients or materials at the quantity you actually use, packaging and labels, a share of utilities, delivery or platform fees, and a fair hourly rate for your own time. Add a margin on top for growth, wastage and the orders that go wrong. If the resulting price feels too high for your market, the answer is usually to simplify the product or batch production, not to work for free.

Review prices every few months as ingredient costs move, and keep a record of every order. Those records become your evidence when you apply for financing from the Social Development Bank or open a business account, and they are what tell you when you are approaching the VAT threshold. Scenarios

A mother baking celebration cakes. She obtains a productive family certificate online, prepares her kitchen for the municipal inspection, and sells through Instagram. Because cakes are made to order, she sets out her cancellation terms clearly: custom-made products are an exception to the seven-day cancellation right.

A graphic designer leaving salaried work. He issues a freelance document under the design profession, uploads his portfolio as the required attachment, and shares the verification link with corporate clients who need proof of status before paying invoices.

A perfume blender scaling up. Sales through her authenticated online store grow steadily. When she wants to hire two employees and supply a department store, she registers an LLC, moves production into a licensed space and begins monthly VAT threshold tracking.

Common mistakes

  • Taking payments into a personal account, which blocks store authentication and worries customers.
  • Choosing the freelance document for an activity that is not on the platform’s list.
  • Preparing food in a shared household space that will not pass inspection.
  • Forgetting renewals.
  • Ignoring the VAT thresholds as sales grow.

Frequently asked questions

Can I legally run a business from home in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Saudi citizens can formalise a home business through the productive families framework, with a Social Development Bank certificate and, where needed, a municipal home-based licence, or sell services under an HRSD freelance document.

What is a productive family certificate?

An online certificate from the Social Development Bank that formalises a home-based business, grants the regulatory licence to practise the activity and qualifies the family for financing, development services and consultation.

Who can get a freelance document in Saudi Arabia?

Saudi citizens aged 18 to 60, for professions listed on the freelance platform. It is valid for one year, renewable, and HRSD lists the service as free.

Do I need a licence to sell food from home?

Home food businesses operate under the productive families framework. Municipal home-based licences involve a site inspection of the preparation space. Check your municipality’s service and Balady’s activity requirements before you start.

Can I sell online with a freelance document?

Yes. The Business Platform authenticates online stores holding a valid CR or freelance document, provided the store has a linked commercial bank account.

Do home businesses pay VAT?

The same thresholds apply as for any business: registration is mandatory above SAR 375,000 of annual taxable supplies and optional from SAR 187,500.

Can a non-Saudi resident start a home business?

The productive family and freelance document routes described here are designed for Saudi citizens. Foreign residents generally need a full business set-up, starting with MISA registration, before trading.

How long is a freelance document valid?

One year from issue, renewable for similar periods. HRSD lists the service as free, and your dashboard on the freelance platform shows the document status and expiry.
