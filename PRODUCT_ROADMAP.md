# AboutFinancials product roadmap

## Product promise

AboutFinancials helps a general reader understand economies, public companies, and practical business economics using traceable primary sources, transparent calculations, and plain-language interpretation.

## Information architecture

1. **Economies:** countries, indicators, comparisons, markets, and the economic calendar.
2. **Company Intelligence:** business models, financial performance, capital allocation, filing notes, commitments, risks, and monitored questions.
3. **Business Plan Lab:** location-specific sector models, unit economics, capital requirements, working capital, break-even, and scenarios.
4. **Tools:** calculators, country comparisons, company comparison tables, and reusable model outputs.
5. **Learn:** structured financial learning paths and reviewed pictorial explanations.

## Editorial primitives

Company research separates reported facts, AboutFinancials calculations, interpretation, and scenarios. Each figure keeps the filing, period, table or note, raw value, unit, displayed rounding, retrieval date, and review state.

Business plans separate external evidence, owner assumptions, and model results. No generic industry average is used without a named source and a compatible geography, date, definition, and scale.

## Delivery sequence

### Phase 1 — foundation

- Preserve and improve the country and indicator source ledger.
- Establish reusable Company Intelligence and Business Plan Lab page patterns.
- Keep navigation among the existing economic pages and the new verticals obvious on mobile and desktop.
- Publish the complete Amazon pilot brief; retain Alphabet and Apple as the next reviewed company briefs.
- Pilot plan structures for a broiler chicken farm and a restaurant; select a geography before adding figures.

### Phase 2 — useful depth

- Add complete company briefs with filing-to-filing revision history.
- Add country comparisons using compatible series and aligned periods.
- Publish the first sourced, editable sector models.
- Add reviewed learning paths and calculators tied to the surrounding explanations.

### Phase 3 — retention

- Saved comparisons and watchlists.
- Filing, indicator, and calendar alerts.
- CSV, image, and citation-ready exports.
- Embeddable country and company cards.

### Phase 4 — paid workflows

- Company filing-change tracker and deep-dive models.
- Downloadable business-plan models and sector benchmark packs.
- Ad-free accounts, team workspaces, and API access.

## Publication gates

No production publication occurs without owner approval. Every release must pass source verification, link and console checks, responsive review, accessibility basics, and editorial review. Diagrams and generated pictorial explanations must be previewed and explicitly approved before inclusion.
